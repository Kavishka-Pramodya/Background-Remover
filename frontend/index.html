How to Use Product Image Studio

1

Upload Your Product Photo

Drag & drop or browse to upload a JPEG, PNG, or WebP image (up to 10 MB). Any background works — the AI will remove it automatically.

2

Choose a Studio Background

Select from premium studio backgrounds designed for professional e-commerce listings. Each template provides a clean, consistent look.

3

Adjust Your Image

Fine-tune the product size, position, and shadow to get the perfect composition. All adjustments are non-destructive.

4

Download

Download your finished image as a high-quality PNG (with transparency support) or optimised JPEG. Ready for your product listings.
